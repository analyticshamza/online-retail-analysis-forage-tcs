# Online Retail Performance Analysis & Forecasting

A comprehensive analysis of online retail sales data to identify revenue trends, seasonality, customer concentration, and geographic demand patterns to support data-driven business decisions.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project combines Excel-based data cleaning with Tableau visual analytics to analyze online retail performance and forecast future growth opportunities. The analysis provides actionable insights for executive-level decision-making through interactive dashboards and predictive modeling.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Identify revenue trends and seasonal patterns
- Analyze customer concentration and revenue dependency
- Evaluate geographic demand distribution
- Forecast future revenue using historical data
- Highlight expansion opportunities in high-potential markets

## 🧹 Data Preparation

### Data Cleaning Process (Excel)

The raw dataset underwent rigorous validation and cleaning:

1. **Validation Framework**
   - Created a comprehensive check table to validate data integrity
   - Implemented IF-based logic to flag invalid records

2. **Data Quality Filters**
   - Removed transactions with negative quantities
   - Eliminated records with negative unit prices
   - Excluded entries with missing customer IDs

3. **Output**
   - Generated a clean, analysis-ready retail dataset

## 📊 Analysis & Visualizations

### Tableau Dashboard Components

#### 1. Monthly Revenue Trend & Forecast
- Visualizes revenue patterns over time
- Identifies seasonal peaks and troughs
- Projects future revenue using time-series forecasting

#### 2. Top 10 Countries by Revenue & Quantity
- Highlights high-performing international markets
- Reveals geographic expansion opportunities
- Compares revenue vs. quantity metrics

#### 3. Top 10 Customers by Revenue
- Identifies key revenue contributors
- Assesses customer concentration risk
- Supports customer retention strategies

#### 4. Geographic Distribution of Demand
- Interactive filled map showing global demand
- Visualizes regional performance patterns
- Identifies underserved markets

## 📈 Key Business Insights

| Insight | Business Impact |
|---------|----------------|
| **Seasonal Revenue Pattern** | Strong Q4 peak indicates opportunity for targeted marketing campaigns |
| **International Growth Potential** | Several non-UK markets show high demand, suggesting expansion opportunities |
| **Balanced Customer Base** | Revenue well-distributed across customers, minimizing dependency risk |
| **Positive Forecast Trajectory** | Historical trends indicate continued upward revenue momentum |

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Microsoft Excel 2016 or later
- Tableau Desktop 2020.1 or later

### Usage
1. Clone this repository
2. Open `Online Retail Dataset.xlsx` to review the cleaning process
3. Load the online_retail_cleaned dataset into Tableau
4. Open `Online Retail Performance Analysis & Forecasting.twbx` to explore the visualizations
